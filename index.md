# Alice Lacan, Ph.D.

Junior AI Fellow · CBIO, Mines Paris - PSL & Institut Curie
{: .hero-role}

I am currently a Junior AI Fellow, funded by a 3-year PR[AI]RIE AI Fellowship. I work with [Eloïse Berson](https://elo-nsrb.github.io/eloiseberson/team.html) at the Centre for Computational Biology ([CBIO](https://www.minesparis.psl.eu/cbio/)) at Mines Paris - PSL & Institut Curie (unit U1331), while also teaching in the International Bachelor of Science in AI (IBSAI) from Univ. PSL. 

I work on generative modelling and more general deep learning approaches for **biomedical data**, from epidemiologic data, gene expression profiles to longitudinal electronic health records (EHRs). I have expertise in **scarce, irregular and high-dimensional medical data**, such as tabular data or long token sequences for instance.

From 2021 to 2024, I completed my **PhD in Computer Science** at Univ. Paris-Saclay, in the Arobas team (IBISC) & TAU team (Inria). My thesis focused on generating synthetic transcriptomics (bulk-RNA data) via deep generative models (VAEs, GANs, diffusion) for downstream data augmentation and cancer classification tasks.
